tubectl

tubectl is a private command-line tool for managing YouTube channels. Its only user is the operator, SEONGGYU KIM, who uses it to publish and manage self-produced videos on YouTube channels that the operator owns or manages.

What it does

  • Uploads finished video files from the operator's computer to the operator's own channels (videos.insert) and sets custom thumbnails (thumbnails.set).
  • Edits the title, description, tags, category, and privacy status of the operator's own videos, and schedules or cancels their publication time (videos.update).
  • Lists the operator's own uploads and checks their processing and privacy status (videos.list, playlistItems.list, channels.list), and deletes the operator's own videos on explicit request (videos.delete).
  • Planned: managing playlists, replying to and moderating comments on the operator's own videos, and viewing performance reports for the operator's own channels (YouTube Analytics API).

What it does not do

  • It is not offered to other people. It has no sign-up, no web service, and no other users.
  • It does not access, collect, or analyze data from channels the operator does not own or manage.
  • It does not download YouTube content and does not use the API for scraping.

How it works

The operator runs the tool from a terminal on the operator's own computer. To add a channel, Google's OAuth consent screen asks the operator to sign in and authorize that channel. The tool keeps the resulting token in a local file on that computer, one per channel, and uses it only to perform the actions the operator runs.
